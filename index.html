PART 0

Basic Information

The SPARKS Compass Passport documents students' growth through authentic project-based experiences. It focuses on the development of key capabilities beyond academic learning, encouraging students to explore real-world issues, collaborate with others, and reflect on their personal growth.

PART 1

Ability Self-Assessment

📖 Mastery Scale Guide (click to expand)
1 – Not observed: I have not yet demonstrated this ability in my project.
2 – Novice: I am beginning to try this ability and need guidance.
3 – Learner: I sometimes demonstrate this ability during activities.
4 – Practitioner: I consistently demonstrate this ability independently.
5 – Expert: I apply this ability confidently and actively support others.

SA — Self & Agency
I can set personal learning goals and proactively seek resources to complete tasks.
Alignment with UWC: Self-Management, Project Management (personal level)
CL — Collaboration & Leadership
I listen to others' perspectives and work toward balanced solutions when conflicts arise.
Alignment with UWC: Social Skills, Communication Skills, Pedagogical Skills
GS — Global Citizenship & Service
I am aware of global issues and reflect on how my actions can positively impact my community.
Alignment with UWC: Project Management (action level), Digital Skills
TR — Thinking & Reflection
I use different thinking frameworks to analyse problems and objectively evaluate my strengths and areas for improvement.
Alignment with UWC: Thinking Skills, Research Skills
